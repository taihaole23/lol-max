|strategy =
* {{ii|Rabadon's Deathcap}} is most suited for champions that have effects that scale with ability power (such as abilities, items, and runes), especially those with higher ratios.
* {{ii|Rabadon's Deathcap}} is best purchased after having acquired a high amount of ability power.
** This is because the item does not provide anything other than ability power in contrast to other items which provide unique bonus effects.
*** Those items may scale with and also grant ability power, thus {{ii|Rabadon's Deathcap}} becomes more valuable as it will make those items even more effective due to its large increase in the stat.
* Given a situation where the user can purchase {{ii|Rabadon's Deathcap}} or {{ii|Void Staff}}, the choice between the two is highly dependent on the champion's ratios.
** {{ii|Rabadon's Deathcap}} becomes a better purchase if the user would gain more damage from the large increase in ability power due to having high ability power ratios. Otherwise, {{ii|Void Staff}} is more optimal of a purchase as its passive will increase overall magic damage dealt in spite of ability power ratios.
* While it has the potential to be the single best source of ability power in the game, {{ii|Rabadon's Deathcap}} is rarely purchased first, as its passive ability requires other ability power items to have maximum effectiveness, thus at low ability power it loses a lot of its potential cost effectiveness. It has the most difficult recipe of any item in the game. In addition, other items provide unique passives beyond a statistic that may prove to be much more useful than only gaining ability power. These facts render the item ineffective or even detrimental to build earlier in the game, where the arms race for useful purchases at convenient timings is the most important.
|trivia =
* Upon applying a {{cai|Living Forge|Ornn|Masterwork}} upgrade to a {{ii|Rabadon's Deathcap}} owned by {{ci|Aurora}}, the item will transform into a [[File:Witch's Hat item.png|20px]] ''Witch's Hat''. The upgraded item will have a new icon and a caption that reads: ''"'Friend.' Yes, it is a funny word. Yet perhaps I understand its purpose."''
** It is also called ''Rabbitdon's (Deathcap)'', or ''weirding witch hat'' in the game files.
** This was added in [[V14.12]], two patches before {{cis|Aurora}} release. During these two patches, {{ci|Ornn}} would also receive the flavor item.
* This item resulted from a separation of the offensive and defensive abilities of {{ii|Zhonya's Ring}}. This is the offensive item, while {{ii|Zhonya's Hourglass}} is its defensive counterpart.
* It was originally named Banksy's Deathcap in the Patch Day Preview. This is a tribute to David 'Banksy' Banks, a [[Riot Games Inc.]] Director of Platform Engineering.
* The image artwork would seem to be based on the {{wikia|harrypotter|Sorting Hat}} from the Harry Potter franchise. There are eyes and a mouth-like band visible on the hat, very similar to the Sorting Hat.
