{{as|{{sti|'''Movement speed'''}}|ms}} (also ''Move speed''), or {{as|{{sti|'''MS'''}}|ms}}, is a [[Champion statistic|stat]] that represents the rate at which an entity travels across the [[map]], measured in game-distance units traveled per second.{{TOCright}}
* ''As a frame of reference, {{cis|Teemo}} hat is circa 100 game-distance units in diameter. A champion with 300 movement speed is able to traverse three {{ci|Teemo|Teemos}} standing hat-to-hat next to one another in one second.''

Each champion begins the game with a base amount of movement speed that ranges strictly from {{ccd|Anivia|ms}} (e.g. {{ci|Anivia}}) to {{ccd|Master Yi|ms}} (e.g. {{ci|Master Yi}}). Some effects ''innately'' decrease or increase certain champions' own movement speed, such as {{ci|Kled}} with {{ccd|Kled|ms}} movement speed while {{ai|Dismounted Skaarl the Cowardly Lizard|Kled|dismounted}}.

This base movement speed can be increased by flat bonuses and percentage bonuses, or reduced by [[Types of Crowd Control#Slow|slows]]. These increases or decreases can be sourced from [[Champion ability|champion abilities]], [[item]]s, [[summoner spell]]s, [[rune]]s and [[buff]]s or [[debuff]]s.

== Calculations ==
A champion's total movement speed is given by the following formula:
: (Base MS + Flat MS bonuses){{times}}(1 + Sum of all Additive Percent MS bonuses){{times}}[ Product of (1 + each Multiplicative Movement Speed bonus) ]{{times}}(1 - Highest Slow ratio)
Add any flat bonuses to the champion's base movement speed; the total flat movement speed will be the baseline for the percentage bonuses. Add all additive percentage bonuses together, add 100% to the result, and multiply it with the total flat movement speed. Then, if applicable, apply the slow reduction and any special [[#Multiplicative speed boosts|multiplicative bonuses]] one at a time. If the result is over 415 or under 220 movement speed, apply <u>soft caps</u> as described <u>below</u>.

=== Movement speed caps ===
When the raw movement speed is greater than 415, there are two soft caps applied:
* The raw speed between 415 and 490 gets multiplied by 80%.
* The raw speed over 490 gets multiplied by 50%.
The two ranges have their reductions applied at the same time before being added back together.
: ''As an example, if the calculated raw speed is 600 before soft caps kick in, it is reduced to (600{{minus}}490){{times}}{{fd|0.5}}{{plus}}(490{{minus}}415){{times}}{{fd|0.8}}{{plus}}415{{equals}}530, which means an '''{{fd|11.7%}} reduction of the raw movement speed.'''''

These are the simplified formulas:
* If the raw speed is 415 or lower, no cap is applied.
* If the raw speed is between 415 and 490, final speed is soft capped to (Raw MS){{times}}{{fd|0.8}}{{plus}}{{tt|83|415*(1-0.8)}}.
* If the raw speed is above 490, final speed is soft capped to (Raw MS){{times}}{{fd|0.5}}{{plus}}{{tt|230|415*(1-0.5) + (490-415)*(0.8-0.5)}}.

When the raw movement speed is less than 220 another two soft caps are used:
* If the raw speed is below 220, final speed is increased to {{tt|110|220*(1-0.5)}}{{plus}}(Raw MS){{times}}{{fd|0.5}}.
* If the raw speed is below 0, final speed is increased to {{tt|110|220*(0.5-0)}}{{plus}}(Raw MS){{times}}{{fd|0.01}}.
: ''Example 1: If the calculated raw speed is 180 before soft caps kick in, it is increased to 180{{times}}{{fd|0.5}}{{plus}}110{{equals}}200.''
: ''Example 2: If the calculated raw speed is 0 before soft caps kick in, it is increased to 0{{times}}{{fd|0.5}}{{plus}}110{{equals}}110.''
: ''Example 3: If the calculated raw speed is -10000 before soft caps kick in, it is increased to -10000{{times}}{{fd|0.01}}{{plus}}110{{equals}}10.''

Only the slow with the highest value is taken into account within movement speed calculations. All additional slows are ignored until the higher-valued slow wears off.<!--movement speed self mods should scale multiplicatively with slows just like speed buffs; pending for test-->

=== Slow Resist ===
{{main|Slow resist}}
Slow Resist is a statistic that reduces the strength of {{tip|slows}} by a percentage.

:''Example: 50% Slow Resist reduces a 40% Slow to a 20% Slow. If it would normally reduce e.g. 400 raw speed to 400''{{Times}}''(1{{minus}}{{fd|0.4}}){{equals}}240 raw speed, because of the Slow Resist it reduces 400 raw speed to 400''{{Times}}''(1{{minus}}{{fd|0.4}}{{times}}(1{{minus}}{{fd|0.5}})){{equals}}320 raw speed. If the resulting raw values were beyond the soft cap thresholds, they would each be adjusted as usual.''

== Multiplicative speed boosts ==
Most speed boosts {{tip|stacks additively|stack additively}}, but a select few {{tip|stacks multiplicatively|stack multiplicatively}} with other speed boosts. These do not stack additionally with other percentage movement speed bonus, but rather multiply the '''total''' movement speed after all other percentage movement speed have been calculated. Movement speed obtained thanks to the formula is still affected by movement speed caps (see above).

== Decreasing movement speed ==
There are many ways to decrease a champion's movement speed, with the most usual one being through champion abilities. For a full list of slows, see [[Types of Crowd Control/Sources#Slow]].

Movement speed can also be decreased by negative buff values (e.g. by having a negative [[stat]] scaling with it) down to 0 (ignoring the {{as|{{sti|110 movement speed}}}} floor that {{tip|slows}} have) down to 0, at which point the unit will {{tt|not move|Distinct from imobilizing crowd control, as the unit can still input movement commands and use movement spells}} at all. Movement speed cannot be reduced below 0 in any way.

== Old slow stacking ==
'''Since patch [[V5.13]], units can only be affected by the highest slow value during its effect. Any applied slows that have a lower potency do not further affect the slow amount.'''

Prior to patch [[V5.13]] slow stacked:

Slowing effects followed particular rules when stacking:

* If a champion was affected by multiple slows, the strongest one would be fully applied, while the others were applied sequentially with '''65% reduced effectiveness''' down to 35% of its original strength.

* Slow effects derived from items with the same name or [[named item effect]] did '''NOT''' stack, even when coming from different champions. Only the strongest item slow was applied at any moment. The exception to this was the active effect of {{ii|Randuin's Omen}} which stacked with other slowing items normally, because they were considered champion abilities for slow stacking purposes.

* Attacking a champion with the {{bi|Crest of Cinders}} [[buff]] when the target was already affected by that slow would reapply the debuff, resetting its duration. It would '''NOT''' increase the slow amount. The same held true for the {{si|Exhaust}} [[summoner spell]].

==== Example ====

A champion with a raw movement speed of 400 was affected by a 2 second long 40% slow and a 5 second long 20% slow at the same time.

* For the first two seconds, they would have (1{{minus}}{{fd|0.4}}){{times}}(1{{minus}}{{fd|0.2}}{{times}}{{fd|0.35}}){{equals}}{{fd|0.6}}{{times}}{{fd|0.93}}{{equals}}{{fd|55.8%}} of his original movement speed (a {{fd|44.2%}} effective slow). So he would have 400{{times}}{{fd|0.558}}{{equals}}{{fd|223.2}} raw movement speed.

* For the following three seconds, the effective slow would be 20% and so he would have 400{{times}}{{fd|0.8}}{{equals}}320 movement speed.

* After that, he would return to his normal speed of 400.

=== Infinite MS Theory ===
There is a theory that allow 2 players, one from each team, to reach infinite amount of movement speed. It require the same set-up as seen just before, twice, each per team, but with a {{ci|Malphite}} replacing the {{ci|Rammus}}. The theory is simple: {{sbc|Team 1}} speeds up '''Malphite 1''', and as soon as he reaches his highest amount '''Malphite 2''' steals '''Malphite 1''' movement speed with {{ai|Seismic Shard|Malphite}}, and get speeds up by {{sbc|Team 2}}. Then as soon as he reaches his highest amount '''Malphite 1''' steals '''Malphite 2''' movement speed with {{ai|Seismic Shard|Malphite}} and get speeds up by {{sbc|Team 1}}. Repeat the operation an infinite amount of time and in the end '''Malphite 1''' or '''Malphite 2''' will have an infinite amount of movement speed. As seen in this [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6TSV76fa-j0 video].
