* {{sbc|Ever Rising Moon}} deals {{tip|proc damage}}, and thus will not trigger {{tip|spell effects}}.<!--tested with cheap shot-->
* {{sbc|Ever Rising Moon's}} damage is affected by {{tip|on-hit}} modifiers.{{bug|2}}
* {{sbc|Ever Rising Moon's}} damage by itself is not blocked by {{tip|spell shield}}.
** The damage is blocked if the effect that applied the second stack was blocked by a spell shield.
* Stacks of {{sbc|Ever Rising Moon}} are generated before the damage instances that cause them.
* {{tip|Blind}}, {{tip|Cripple}}, {{tip|Drowsy}}, {{tip|Kinematics}}, {{tip|Nearsight}}, and {{tip|Stasis}} do not count as valid crowd control for triggering stacks of {{sbc|Ever Rising Moon}}.
* Effects that deal {{tip|proc damage}} do not trigger a stack of {{sbc|Ever Rising Moon}} unless it is also tagged as {{tip|pet damage}}.
** Such effects can still trigger a stack if they apply a debuff (i.e {{ii|Black Cleaver}}).
* {{sbc|Ever Rising Moon}} will deal its bonus damage to the closest enemy champion if multiple champions are hit at the same time.
* {{cais|R|Ambessa}} is special cased to apply a stack from {{sbc|Ever Rising Moon}} for the suppression application and the damage dealt.
* {{cai|Razor Shuriken|Zed}} and {{ai|Shadow Slash|Zed}}, when used together with {{ai|Living Shadow|Zed|Shadows}}, are special cased to apply a stack from {{sbc|Ever Rising Moon}} for each tick of damage.
