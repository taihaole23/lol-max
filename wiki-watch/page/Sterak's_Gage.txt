|trivia =
* This item is related to Czech YouTuber and writer "Radek Starý" (known as [https://www.youtube.com/user/tensterakdary Sterakdary]) for achieving high number of [[Refer-A-Friend|referrals]]. Although the newer referral system was available, Sterakdary was still using the original referral system.
* {{sbc|The Claws that Catch}} references {{w|Jabberwocky}}.
* The item's icon was recolored for the icon of the [[Teamfight Tactics]] item {{TFTi|Trap Claw}}, from red to blue.
* The search term 'primal' references a precursor item from development: ''Primal Talon''.
