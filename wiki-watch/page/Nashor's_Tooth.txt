* '''Nashor's Tooth''' is best used by champions that can benefit from the on-hit damage and bonus attack speed, mainly magic damage dealers that use basic attacks in addition to their abilities.
* '''Nashor's Tooth's''' ability power scaling allows its users to build ability power that increases both the damage dealt by their attacks via the item and their abilities simultaneously.
