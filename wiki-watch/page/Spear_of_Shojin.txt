|trivia =
* This item is a reference to the [[Shojin Monastery]] and the [[Shojin Order]] of [[Ionia]].
* {{sbc|Awakened Dragon}} might have been a reference to {{ci|Lee Sin}} since he has the ability to channel the "''dragon’s spirit''" and he arrived at the Shojin Monastery at a young age.
* The name {{sbc|Dragonforce}} may potentially be referencing {{w|DragonForce}}.
* '''Spear of Shojin''' was first introduced on the PBE for the patch [[V8.12]], but was delayed multiple times, until it was released with [[Nexus Blitz]].
* The {{sbc|Awakened Dragon}} passive was previously on {{ii|Essence Reaver}} before patch [[V9.3]].
