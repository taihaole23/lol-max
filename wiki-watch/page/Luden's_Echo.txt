|trivia =
* This item was designed to replace the removed {{ii|Deathfire Grasp}} as the third high-end AP item.
* The name "Luden's Echo" was data-mined from the [[VPBE|PBE]] update pushed on 2015-02-17.<ref>http://www.surrenderat20.net/2015/02/217-pbe-update.html</ref> The item was later enabled for testing with placeholder name "Item 3285".
* The name "Luden's Echo" comes from a player who reached 1000 referrals on the old [[Refer-A-Friend]] system.<ref>http://www.reddit.com/r/leagueoflegends/comments/2ysdns/who_is_luden_and_why_does_he_get_an_item_named/cpcukll</ref>
* During testing on the [[VPBE|PBE]], the item's icon was a purple recolor of {{ii|Statikk Shiv}}.
