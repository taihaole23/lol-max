|trivia =
* '''Trinity Force''' features the most champion statistics on one item.
* {{sbc|Spellblade's}} highest possible damage is {{tt|{{fd|{{#expr:{{Highest lowest stats|ad|lvl=18|sortby=top|size=1|get=stat}}*2}}}}|{{Highest lowest stats|ad|lvl=18|sortby=top|size=1|get=stat}}{{times}}2}}. This can be achieved by {{Highest lowest stats|ad|lvl=18|sortby=top|size=1|get=champ}}, who has {{as|{{fd|{{Highest lowest stats|ad|lvl=18|sortby=top|size=1|get=stat}}}} '''base''' attack damage}} at level 18.
* The name and icon of '''Trinity Force''' are inspired by ''The Legend of Zelda's'' [https://zeldawiki.wiki/wiki/Triforce Triforce].
* '''Trinity Force''' can be found by typing in the item search:
** 'tons of damage', which references [[David 'Phreak' Turley]]'s catchphrase in [[Champion Spotlight]]s as well as the meme around it.
*** The "''Recommended Items''" section of the item [[shop]] (before the [[Season 2021|Preseason 2021]] update) also displayed "''Tons of Damage''" as the description for '''Trinity Force''' when shown as a recommended item for a champion.
** 'triforce', referencing {{w|Legend of Zelda}}.
** '3333', the item's price has remained {{g|3333}} since [[V10.23]], referencing the "trinity" element of the item.
* The swords in the original icon resemble {{ii|Sheen}} with the two swords of {{ii|Zeal}}, which were part of its build path.
* The blades in the icon ([[V10.23]]) resemble {{cis|Irelia}} blades, which are fragments of an Ionian Crest.
