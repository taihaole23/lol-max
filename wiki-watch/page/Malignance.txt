* {{sbc|Hatefog}} deals {{tip|proc damage}} and is tagged as {{tip|persistent damage|periodic}} and {{tip|area damage|area of effect}} without applying {{tip|spell effects}}.
* {{sbc|Hatefog}} is not blocked by {{tip|spell shield}}.
* {{sbc|Hatefog}} does not trigger from [[Damage#0_Damage_Instances|0 damage instances]] nor against {{tip|invulnerable}} champions.
* {{sbc|Hatefog}} is an [[auto-targeted]] effect.
* {{sbc|Hatefog's}} magic resistance reduction does not linger and is removed as soon as the target loses the ''Curse'' debuff.
* Assuming a target has not been afflicted by ''Curse'', the first instance of magic damage from an ultimate ability does not benefit from {{sbc|Hatefog's}} magic resistance reduction.
* {{sbc|Hatefog}} will trigger on each instance of ultimate ability damage dealt (see list below for special interactions).
** The zone can only be created against the same target once every 3 seconds. This timer starts as soon as the zone is spawned.
*** Ultimate ability damage against champions already inside of a zone will instead refresh the timer on it, but only if the damaged champion is the "owner" of that zone.
** Enemies can only be ''Cursed'' by one zone at a time; they can only be damaged and have their magic resistance reduced by one zone.
** {{sbc|Hatefog}} will not create a zone against enemies that are already ''Cursed'' by one.
*** If multiple targets are close to one another and are damaged at the same time, {{sbc|Hatefog}} will create a zone underneath the first champion damaged which is based on {{tt|champion spawn order|Order in which champions are spawned in the game; Hatefog will prioritize the first enemy champion spawned}}.
** Each scorch zone has its own radius relative to the amount of damage dealt for each instance.
** The zone deals a tick of damage instantly to enemies surrounding the primary target.
