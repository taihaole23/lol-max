|trivia =
* '''Blackfire Torch's''' passive effect is similar to that of {{ii|Liandry's Torment}}.
** {{ii|Liandry's Torment}} however did not exist at the time of when '''Blackfire Torch''' was added.
*** On [[V3.10]], {{ii|Liandry's Torment}} was enabled on the [[Twisted Treeline (League of Legends)|Twisted Treeline]] and [[Crystal Scar (League of Legends)|Crystal Scar]], replacing '''Blackfire Torch's''' original functionality.
* '''Blackfire Torch''' was revamped in [[V3.10]] as sort of a lesser version of {{ii|Deathfire Grasp}}, which was disabled on the Twisted Treeline and Crystal Scar on the same patch. '''Blackfire Torch''' was {{g|450}} cheaper at the time with lower stats to compensate, and also had a damage over time effect instead of a high damage burst effect.
* The visual and sound effects of '''Blackfire Torch's''' old active was the same as {{iis|Deathfire Grasp}} except the VFX was recolored to green.
* Bots were able to purchase this item even though it was removed from the game. This was later fixed at some point after the removal.<ref>[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6mwH2nEFx6c Bots using the item, recorded on V6.9]</ref>
