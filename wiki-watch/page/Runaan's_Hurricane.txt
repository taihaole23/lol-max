[[File:Runaan's Hurricane hitbox.png|right|280px]]
* {{sbc|Wind's Fury}} deals {{tip|basic damage}} and is tagged as {{tip|area damage|area of effect}} without applying {{tip|spell effects}}.
* {{sbc|Wind's Fury}} is an [[auto-targeted]] effect.
* {{sbc|Wind's Fury's}} bolts:
** Deal the same damage type and apply the same hit effects as basic attacks would.
*** This means that they {{tip|applies lifesteal|apply}} {{stil|life steal}}, trigger [[on-hit effects]] (excluding {{cai|W|Vayne}}), can {{tip|critically strike}}, draw {{tip|minion}} aggro and can be parried by effects such as {{tip|dodge}}, {{tip|block}}, and {{tip|blind}}.
**** They will only critically strike if the primary attack would do so (with the exception of guaranteed critical strikes).
**** They do not trigger [[on-attack effects]].
** They are modified by some effects that modify basic attacks (e.g {{cai|Infernum|Aphelios}}, {{cai|Q|Jinx}}, {{cai|Q|Yunara}}), but not by others (e.g {{cai|I|Graves}}, {{cai|R|Twitch}}, {{cai|W|Sivir}}). {{supNote|The design choice is likely on a case by case basis to prevent runaan aoe from increasing single target damage}}
** Are blockable {{tip|projectile|projectiles}}.
** Have a missile speed of 2000.
*** Of the unique override variants, {{cais|Q|Ashe}} and {{cai|Basic Attack|Kalista}} missiles are the only ones with a different speed of 2500.
** Does not target [[structure]]s nor [[ward]]s.
** Deals {{as|0 physical damage}} on-hit to the primary target that is able to stack {{iis|Black Cleaver}} {{nie|Carve}}.
* {{sbc|Wind's Fury's}} damage is unaffected if the primary attack deals modified damage, with the exceptions of {{cai|I|Ashe}} and {{ai|Q|Ashe}}, {{cai|Infernum|Aphelios}}, {{cai|I|Corki}} and {{cai|Switcheroo!|Jinx|Fishbones}}.
* {{sbc|Wind's Fury}} will not trigger effects that were already consumed by the primary attack, but will do so if they are not consumed.
* [[Shapeshifter|Shapeshifting]] champions can purchase '''Runaan's Hurricane''' but cannot trigger {{sbc|Wind's Fury}} without being in their {{tip|ranged}} form.
* The movement speed stat stacks additively with other percentage movement speed modifiers.
|strategy =
* {{ii|Runaan's Hurricane}} is most effective when paired with on-hit effects that can be applied through its bolts.
* {{ii|Runaan's Hurricane}} can also be used to increase the effectiveness of life steal since the bolts benefit from the stat with their damage.
* {{ii|Runaan's Hurricane}} is highly effective in taking down multiple enemies at a time with its bolts.

|trivia =
* Runaan's Hurricane is named after [[Devon 'Runaan' Giehl]], an Associate Creative Designer at Riot Games.
* {{ii|Runaan's Hurricane}} is actually a small floating orb that accompanies you around the battlefield, functioning similar to {{cai|Pix, Faerie Companion|Lulu}}.
* The effect's name '''Wind's Fury''' is a reference to [https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Windfury_Weapon Windfury Weapon], a Shaman spell in '''World of Warcraft'''.
* The item may have been inspired by the [[alpha items|alpha item]] {{ii|Hurricane|nolink=true}}, which was also a bow, though Hurricane's Alpha effect is unknown.
* In {{w|Minecraft|Minecraft's}} [https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/ Hypixel SkyBlock] server, there are two items named after '''Runaan's Hurricane''': the [https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/Hurricane_Bow Hurricane Bow] and the [https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/Runaan%27s_Bow Runaan's Bow].
