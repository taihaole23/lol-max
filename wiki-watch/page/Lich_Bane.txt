|strategy =
* In comparison to other {{sbc|Spellblade}} items, {{ii|Lich Bane}} is a more appropriate choice for magic damage dealers that also use basic attacks.
** Champions that are hybrid, both physical and magic damage focused, may prefer {{ii|Lich Bane}} over {{ii|Trinity Force}} as it scales much better due to also having a scaling with ability power.
* {{iis|Lich Bane}} scaling with ability power separates it from {{ii|Trinity Force}} as it does not necessarily need to be utilized by champions with high base attack damage to deal the most bonus damage. This does not mean champions with that luxury should not build it, as it does also scale with base attack damage.
