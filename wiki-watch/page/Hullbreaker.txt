|strategy =
* {{ii|Hullbreaker}} is best used to push out lanes and siege [[structures]] with or without your team.
** The damage increase and empowerment to your large {{tip|minions}} will increase the speed of your push significantly.
* {{sbc|Boarding Party}} empowers all nearby large {{tip|minions}}. Use this to your advantage by building up a wave consisting of several large minions.
* {{sbc|Boarding Party's}} empowerment stacks with {{bi|Hand of Baron|Hand of Baron's}} empowerment.
