|strategy =
* {{ii|Wit's End}} is both an offensive and defensive item for magic damage; it provides magic damage on-hit while also resisting magic damage taken.
* {{ii|Wit's End}} can be purchased on physical damage attackers to counter enemies with high amounts of armor with its passive that deals magic damage.
|trivia =
* Previously, '''Wit's End''' gave 42 on-hit magic damage and attack speed. Considering the item's name, this was probably a reference to {{w|The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy}}, where 42 is the answer to "the Ultimate Question of Life, the Universe, and Everything".
* {{ii|Nashor's Tooth}} is the ability power counterpart to {{ii|Wit's End}}.
