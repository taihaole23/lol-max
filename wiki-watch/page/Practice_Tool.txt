'''Practice Tool''' is the sandbox mode of [[League of Legends]]. Released in [[V7.3]], the mode was specifically designed to allow players to interact with training dummies and enable cheats on {{tip|Summoner's Rift}}.

On patch [[V25.S1.1]], Practice Tool was granted support for multiplayer, allowing up to 10 players (5v5) within the lobby. Prior to this patch and since its release, it could only be played by a single player with the option of enabling only one bot on the enemy team (1v1).

== General ==
* {{sbc|Map}}: {{tip|Summoner's Rift}}
* {{sbc|Draft type}}: [[Blind pick]]
* {{sbc|Mode:}} {{tip|Classic 5v5}}

A simple, straight forward environment that lets you and other players test out things like checking wall {{si|Flash}} points or if {{ui|Dragon}} can be slain solo with a certain item build, without having to manually spend time experimenting in [[Custom game]]s.

== Bots ==
[[File:Custom game bot options.png|thumb|320px|Bot selection interface in the Practice Tool lobby.]]
Up to 9 bots can be added between teams in the lobby to participate in the practice session. Each bot's intelligence can be configured to the lobby owner's desire, ranking from ''Intro'' to ''Beginner'' to ''Intermediate'', with higher ranks further advancing the bot's AI.

Dummy is a unit type that can be directly summoned in Practice Tool, it stands in as a champion for all intents and purposes. It can be summoned by pressing {{sbc|Shift + B}} for the enemy dummy, or {{sbc|Shift + N}} for the ally dummy. It will face toward the player that summoned it.

Upon taking damage from a champion, the Dummy will track total damage, damage per second, and last tick of damage value. Dummy health can be seen dropped, but they cannot die (similar to {{ais|Undying Rage|Tryndamere}} buff). After 3 seconds of not taking damage (remaining time displayed visually by the dummy's resource bar), the damage tracking is reset and the dummy health is restored to full.

Using practice tool commands, the player can change maximum health and resistances only of an enemy dummy. Dummies gain up to +100% increased size based on {{as|'''bonus''' health}}, with the maximum size capped at 10,000 health (130 gameplay radius). Allied dummies always have the default statistics unless modified by other means.

A dummy's health cannot go below 1 health.

A dummy can take a maximum of ({{as|100% of target dummy's '''maximum''' health}} - 1) damage.

Dummies are immune to {{tip|Grievous Wounds}}.

== Commands ==
[[File:Practice Tool cheat menu.png|300px|right|thumb|Cheat menu interface]]
Commands, or cheats, are functions that help players in testing. It can be a resource free, instant refresh spells so you can practice landing them. Or you can summon jungle camps repetitively to test the best jungle route.

The player may see all of the commands using the cheat menu located to the left of their screen. Commands can be activated either by clicking the respective cheat button in the cheat menu interface, or by pressing the designated hotkeys configured for each one (default hotkey for each command can be seen in the below table).

== Particulars ==
Results in Practice Tool do not always reflect the same circumstances in other game modes or have special interactions with its various cheats.

=== Target dummy ===
The dummy imposes special restrictions on certain abilities in order to avoid disrupting their function.
* {{cai|Fear Beyond Death|Urgot}} can kill a dummy.
** If the dummy is being {{tip|suppression|suppressed}} by {{ai|Fear Beyond Death 2|Urgot|Mercy}}, {{bi|Aspect of the Dragon}} can kill the dummy. {{bi|Aspect of the Dragon}} alone can also kill a dummy in rare occasions.
* {{iis|Sundered Sky}} {{sbc|Lightshield Strike}} does not function against the dummy.{{bug}}
* A killed dummy would respawn in its [[spawn|fountain]].

=== Map ===
* Minion waves spawn every 30 seconds invariably and do not respect current Summoner's Rift rules (30 to 25 to 20 seconds at game time breakpoints).{{bug}}
* [[File:Practice Tool Reset Game.png|26px|link=]] Resetting the game will not change the map's element nor the game's set {{bi|Dragon Soul}}, but will revert the map to its pre-elemental form.
* After killing an {{ui|Dragon|Elemental Drake|link=Dragon pit}}, spawning another too early will make the summoned '''Elemental Drake''' {{tip|untargetable}}.{{bug}}
* Each Elemental Drake can be slain up to 4 times, even after the Dragon Soul has been acquired, to gain the respective {{bi|Dragon Slayer}} bonuses.
** The exception is {{ui|Chemtech Drake}}, which may [[stack]] infinitely.{{bug|2}}
* Every other Dragon (even numbered spawns) spawned by the Practice Tool will be an noninteractive dragon model that idles in place. They stack infinitely.
* The {{ui|Voidgrub|Voidgrubs}} will not re-spawn a full group via the [[File:Practice Tool Spawn Jungle Camps and Grubbies.png|26px|link=]] Spawn Grubbies button if any of the three in the group are alive.
* The {{ui|Voidgrub|Voidgrubs}} can co-exist with either {{ui|Rift Herald}} or {{ui|Baron Nashor}} in the [[baron pit]] (the latter two are mutually exclusive). Once {{ui|Baron Nashor}} spawns, {{ui|Rift Herald}} cannot spawn again for the current session, including through [[File:Practice Tool Reset Game.png|26px|link=]] resets.
** Additionally, if the baron pit camps' possible spawn time has elapsed ('''is reset''' through [[File:Practice Tool Reset Game.png|26px|link=]] resets) {{ui|Rift Herald}} cannot spawn again for the current session.
** If a player attempts to spawn the Rift Herald while Baron Nashor is already spawned beforehand, the Herald is killed instantly.
* Prior to [[V25.S1.1]], after killing the first {{ui|Dragon|Elemental Drake|link=Dragon pit}} of the game, spawning the same one again would set the map's element and the game's {{bi|Dragon Soul}}.{{bug|2}}

=== Other ===
* {{ci|Kled}} will have to use [[File:Practice Tool Kill Player Champion.png|26px|link=]] Kill Player Champion function twice if he is mounted; one to dismount, and one to actually enter death state.
* Using the [[File:Practice Tool Kill Player Champion.png|26px|link=]] Kill Player Champion function during {{cai|The Darkin Scythe|Kayn|Transformation}} will not grant Kayn his new form, abilities nor update the animations accordingly for which form he chose. Moreover, Kayn will not be able to transform unless the game is [[File:Practice Tool Reset Game.png|26px|link=]] reset.
* If {{cai|Spray and Pray|Twitch}} is cast again before the effect has ended, it will refresh the stat buff (bonus AD and bonus range) but '''Twitch''' will fire standard attacks instead of the ''bolts''.
* Casting {{cai|Cease and Desist|Vi}} on two different targets in quick succession will affect both of them at the same time if they are in a certain range of each other.
* Each player can only have up to 20 {{tip|Farsight ward|Farsight wards}} on the map at any given time, with the oldest one being replaced upon a new one spawning over the limit.
* After using [[File:Practice_Tool_Fast_Forward_10min.png|link=|width=26x26]] Fast Forward 10min, certain game mechanics refresh and occur significantly more frequently. For example, the [[File:Practice_Tool_Auto-Refresh_Cooldowns.png|link=|width=26x26]] Auto-Refresh Cooldowns toggle refreshes more quickly, allowing the player to cast abilities even more often. Player gold will also continue to rapidly accumulate for a short period afterward.{{bug}}
* Some {{tip|on-hit}} effects will not trigger against invincible towers.
* Respawned nexus turrets lose their [[File:Practice_Tool_Toggle_Tower_Fire.png|link=|width=26x26]] Toggle Fire status, but their [[File:Practice Tool Toggle Towers Invincible.png|26px|link=]] Invincibility state will remain if toggled after their destruction.{{bug}}
* [[Feats of Strength]] cannot be reset; they are locked for the entirety of the session.
* If the game is [[File:Practice Tool Reset Game.png|26px|link=]] reset while the [[File:Practice_Tool_Toggle_Minion_Spawn.png|link=|width=26x26]] Toggle Minion Spawn cheat is active, the cheat will no longer despawn minions when used. However, it will continue spawning new ones.{{bug}}
* A {{tip|turret}} in an [[File:Practice Tool Toggle Towers Invincible.png|26px|link=]] Invincibility state might still take damage from certain effects.
** This includes but is not limited to: [[Turret#Items|Crystalline Overgrowth]] and {{ri|Demolish}}.
* Some items passives will visually reset but will stay on cooldown even if [[File:Practice_Tool_Auto-Refresh_Cooldowns.png|link=|width=26x26]] Auto-Refresh Cooldowns is toggled.{{bug}}
** These items are: all {{iis|Sheen}} items, all {{iis|Tear of the Goddess}} items, {{ii|Statikk Shiv}} and items whose passive cooldowns are different for each target.

== Trivia ==
* '''Practice Tool''' games that are finished are tracked as [[custom game|custom games]] in [[Summoner_profile#Match_History|Match History]].
* The Training Dummy design is based on {{ci|Teemo}}; red representing an enemy target dummy while the blue an ally target dummy.
* Attacking an {{TFT Item|Target Dummy|Enemy Target Dummy}}, monster, or champion will allow you to more quickly and easily add or remove the target's health or resistances. This is because the game does not require you to select the target after issuing the command.
* After using the practice tool for 59:14, a warning will appear stating that the practice tool will close in 1 minute. This makes the longest time that anyone can use the practice tool in one sitting 60 minutes and 14 seconds.
* Dummies are summoned at level 1 and can gain experience like normal champions. They can also level up, but gain no benefits from it.
* Dummies will face directly towards your location at the time of being summoned.
* When you use the option 'Win the Game', a message appears over your character: "My only way to see that Victory screen."
* In {{tip|Teamfight Tactics}}, the item {{TFT Item|Target Dummy}} reuses the model and animations of the Training Dummy.
