A '''critical strike''' is a [[damage]] event that deals 200% of its normal value by default, denoted as {{sti|{{as|critical strike damage}}}}. This can occur on some [[item]]s and [[Champion ability|abilities]], and almost always on basic attacks. Depending on the effect, a critical strike would occur under specific conditions via [[champion ability|champion abilities]] or [[item|item abilities]] (and either [[passive]]ly or [[active]]ly), or semi-randomly based on {{sti|{{as|critical strike chance}}}}.

Critical strikes interact exclusively with the game mechanics of {{sti|{{as|critical chance}}}}, {{sti|{{as|critical damage}}}}, and {{iis|Randuin's Omen}} {{nie|Resilience}}. The most common form of critical strike occurs on {{tip|basic attack|basic attacks}}. Basic attacks cannot critically strike against [[structures]]. The exceptions are the enhanced attacks from {{cai|E|Fiora}}, {{cai|I|Jhin}} and {{cai|I|Master Yi}}.

Critical strikes are especially valuable for champions who primarily output basic attacks rather than abilities, since champion basic attacks have lower cooldowns compared to most abilities. Some champions, like {{ci|Ashe}}, have an affinity for critical strikes and benefit more than others from items that improve them.

== Critical strike chance ==
{{sti|{{as|'''Critical strike chance'''}}}} (often shortened to ''crit chance'', or ''% crit'' / ''crit%'') is a [[Champion statistic|stat]] that denotes the probability, or '''chance''', that a basic attack will critically strike. Some damaging effects may be able to critically strike using the same chance, some others may [[Champion statistic|scale with]] critical strike chance but are usually not able to critically strike themselves, and some may critically strike without a pre-requisite for this chance but based on other conditions.
* Critical strike chance '''{{tip|stacks additively}}''', and each point acquired directly adds to the statistic (1:1).

All champions start at 0% critical strike chance, and can only increase it via [[item]]s or certain abilities. Critical strike chance cannot exceed 100%, but some effects may be special-cased to benefit from the excess, such as by granting additional statistics (e.g. {{cai|Absolution|Senna}}).

{{gold value}}
* Critical strike chance has a gold value of {{g|{{#invoke:Gold value|wikivaluedefine}}{{#var:crit}}}} per point.

== Algorithm ==
Critical strikes being rolled is not governed by complete RNG but by a pseudorandom algorithm that aims to alleviate full randomness in a manner that is not intrusive. Generally, the probability of a critical strike chance increases with the number of attacks that have not critically struck against the same target, called a failure streak. It is governed by the following rules:
# An internal constant, herein called ''luck modifier'', is explicitly defined every {{fd|0.5}} percent points of critical strike chance (rounded down when applying the user's critical strike chance). When graphed, the values resemble an [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Error_function error function] where the midpoint (the 50% luck modifier mark) corresponds to exactly 63% critical strike chance.
# 20% of the user's '''missing''' critical strike chance is guaranteed to apply every roll. This determines an absolute maximum ''modified'' probability of a critical strike and prevents guaranteed critical strikes at any chance below 100%.
#* For example, at 25% critical strike chance, the maximum modified failure chance is {{fd|0.2}}{{times}}(1{{minus}}{{fd|0.25}}){{equals}}{{fd|0.15}}{{equals}}15%, making the maximum critical chance its 1-complement, 85%.
# Observed critical strike probability is defined as the minimum between [ the maximum modified probability ] and [ the product of the luck modifier and the current streak ].
# Streaks max out their probability at 5. This means any further rolls if unsuccessful will use the same probability as when on a streak of 5.

As a result, the probability of failure streaks noticeably increases at lower luck modifiers, and the probability of critical strike streaks noticeably increases at higher critical strike chance.

When averaged over a high number of basic attacks (i.e. calculating damage per second), critical strike chance is essentially a damage multiplier. At '''base''' critical strike damage (''100% bonus damage''), every 1% of critical chance equates to a 1% damage increase.
: For example, the damage dealt with basic attacks by a champion with 50% critical strike chance and base critical damage is on average 150% of the damage they would deal if they had 0% critical strike chance.

For this reason, critical strike chance scales well with both [[attack damage]] and [[attack speed]]. The formula for this damage multiplier is:
:''Average damage multiplier'' = 1{{plus}}(''Critical chance''{{times}}(1 + '''''Bonus''' critical damage''))

''Critical strikes do not affect structures, with the following exceptions:''
* {{cai|Bladework|Fiora}} - second attack will also critically strike turrets.
* {{cai|Whisper|Jhin}} - the fourth attack will also critically strike turrets.
* {{cai|Double Strike|Master Yi}} - the additional attack can critically strike turrets.{{bug}}

== Interacting with critical strikes ==
* {{cai|Infernum|Aphelios}} sprays 6 missiles in a wider cone when critically striking enemies and {{ai|Crescendum|Aphelios}} has a reduced {{tip|attack windup|wind up}} percentage when critically striking enemies.
* {{cai|New Destiny|Graves}} sprays {{as|6 pellets|buzzword2}} in a wider cone when critically striking enemies.
* {{cai|Whisper|Jhin}} grants him movement speed when critically striking enemies.
* {{cais|Lightning Rush|Kennen}} attack speed duration is extended when critically striking enemies.
* {{cai|Spinning Slash|Tryndamere}} reduces its {{sti|cooldown}} when critically striking enemies.
* {{cai|Last Breath|Yasuo}} grants all critical strikes {{sti|armor penetration|{{as|armor penetration}}}} against {{sti|armor|{{as|'''bonus''' armor}}}}.
* {{cai|Vow of the First Lands|Yunara}} causes critical strikes to deal increased damage (without modifying critical strike damage).
* {{cai|Spark Surge|Zeri}} reduces its {{sti|cooldown}} by an increased amount when {{ai|Burst Fire|Zeri}} critically strikes enemies and {{ai|Lightning Crash|Zeri}} will grant extra stacks when {{ai|Burst Fire|Zeri}} or {{ai|Ultrashock Laser|Zeri}} critically strikes enemies.
* {{iis|Fiendhunter Bolts}} {{nie|Opening Barrage}} deals bonus damage if its empowered attacks would have already critically struck based on the user's critical strike chance.
* {{iis|Randuin's Omen}} [[Named item effect#Resilience|Resilience]] reduces the damage dealt by incoming critical strikes from enemies.

== Trivia ==
* From its inception, critical strikes did 200% damage before being changed to 175% in [[V10.23]]. It would later be reverted to 200% again in [[V26.01]].
* In many MMO settings, a number of attack damage-based assassins or secondary assassin-based champions also benefit from high critical strike chance in varying forms, as a means to help finish off targets faster as well as to deal more damage in bursts overall.
* Most [[champion]]s have special [[basic attack]] animations that play when they critically strike, even champions who may not usually opt into critical strike chance.
* A few champions that typically itemize critical strike chance may have quotes that play when they critically strike.
* Some champions may actually use their critical strike animation for an empowered basic attack, when consuming a single-use on-hit effect ''(this is entirely cosmetic by itself and does not represent an actual critical strike)''.
* At negative attack damage, critical strikes from basic attacks are also multiplied by the same amount (e.g. {{minus}}50 AD &rarr; {{minus}}{{ap|50*2}} critical physical damage).
