|strategy =
* {{ii|Rod of Ages}} is best used by champions that can make use of the large stat increases by scaling with them effectively (usually mana).
* {{ii|Rod of Ages}} is often the item that is built the first by its users due to wanting to stack up its passive as early as possible into the game.
** This is because the stats the item grants by itself along with the stacks are highly valuable, especially in the early game.
** Having the item as part of one's build is too high of an investment since it takes a total of 10 minutes for it to reach its full potential, so it must be prioritized as a rush item so that it achieves that power spike sooner than later.
* {{ii|Rod of Ages}} also provides a slight amount of sustain with its other passive which recovers both health and mana.
|oldicons =
|trivia =
* {{ii|Rod of Ages}} may have been originally named as "Guinsoo's Rod of Oblivion", and its icon is still named as such in the League of Legends install directory.
* {{csl|Karthus|Karthus Lightsbane|Karthus Lightsbane}} uses the {{ii|Rod of Ages}}.
* The name is likely a reference to Def Leppard's 1983 song "Rock of Ages". (The movie of the same name, which is also named after the song, was not released until 3 years after the item came out.)
* The {{ii|Rod of Ages (Quick Charge)|Quick Charge}} version is also used on the [[Crystal Scar]] during [[Rotating Game Mode]]s.
* The name of its Masterwork item on [[VPBE|PBE]] during the Preseason 2023 cycle was ''Scepter of Eternity''.
