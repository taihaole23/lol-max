|notes =
* {{sbc|Wrath}} deals {{tip|proc damage}}, and thus will not trigger [[spell effects]].
* {{sbc|Wrath's}} on-hit damage does not apply to [[structure]]s.
* The bonus damage dealt by {{sbc|Wrath}} benefits from {{stil|life steal}}.
* {{sbc|Seething Strike}} is not blocked by {{tip|spell shield}}.
* Attacks against [[structure]]s will not grant, refresh, or affect the count of ''Phantom'' stacks.
** They will grant and refresh {{sbc|Seething Strike}} stacks.
* The {{sbc|Phantom Hit}} occurs a static delay after the basic attack was fired (independently of {{tip|melee}} or {{tip|ranged}}), and will occur even if the attack was destroyed (i.e. {{ai|Wind Wall|Yasuo}}) or {{tip|homing projectile destruction|popped}} (i.e. {{tip|untargetable|untargetability}}).
** If a ranged attack is still in flight after {{fd|0.15}} seconds, its ''Phantom Hit'' lands first, which can change the order of on-hit effects (e.g. {{ii|Blade of the Ruined King}}).
* The ''Phantom Hit'' from {{sbc|Seething Strike}} may generate stacks and trigger on abilities that applies on-attack effect. <!--Abilities that could hit multiple targets or applies multiple instances of damages only counted as single attack at same time.
Examples: Katarina death lotus will generate a stack per tick and only hit closest target. Zeri, Yone and Yasuo Q will only generate one stack regardless number of enemy hit. Renekton or Pantheon empowered attacks is counted as separated attacks and so on.-->
** On-hit effects, such as those from single-use empowered attacks, will not benefit from ''Phantom Hit'' and will apply their effect only once. This is due to the on-hit effect itself being consumed by the initial strike and unavailable for the second.
** ''Phantom Hit'' does not interact with on-attack effects.

|strategy =
* {{ii|Guinsoo's Rageblade}} stats and effects make it a useful item for when a champion wants to specialize in dealing and applying on-hit effects. It is best used by a champion that is building to use basic attacks for their main source of damage.

|trivia =
* {{ii|Guinsoo's Rageblade}} is named after [[Steve 'Guinsoo' Feak]], one of the main creative minds behind ''[[League of Legends]]'' and creator of ''[[Defense of the Ancients: Allstars]]''.
** According to Feak, '''Guinsoo's Rageblade''' was created with {{ci|Corki}} in mind.<ref>[https://web.archive.org/web/0/http://na.leagueoflegends.com/board/showthread.php?p=2851828#2851828]</ref>
** It has a similar icon to the ''[[Defense of the Ancients: Allstars]]'' item Guinsoo's Scythe of Vyse, also named after Feak.
** Guinsoo is also the name of a mysterious dagger that can be found hidden on Level 1 of the 1991 D&D video game, Eye of the Beholder. Steve Feak took his name from this dagger.<ref>[https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Guinsoo]</ref>
* '''Guinsoo's Rageblade''' was one of the last items released before the launch of ''League of Legends'' itself.
* There once existed a bug that enabled {{ci|Sion|variant = old}} to attain negative {{as|ability power}} and {{as|{{fd|2.5}} attack speed}} with '''Guinsoo's Rageblade''' alone.<ref>[https://web.archive.org/web/0/http://na.leagueoflegends.com/board/showthread.php?t=1506235]</ref>
* The design is mostly similar to Kratos' blades (God of War franchise), both the Blades of Chaos and the Blades of Exile.
