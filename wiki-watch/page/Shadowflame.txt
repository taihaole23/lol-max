* {{sbc|Cinderbloom}} uses the following formula to calculate the damage for its critical strike: <math>{\rm increased\ damage}={\rm base\ damage}+(({\rm base\ damage}\times 0.2)\times ({\rm product\ of\ all\ critical\ damage\ modifiers}))</math>.
** For example, if an ability deals 100 damage by default, then it will critically strike for 120 damage: <math>{\rm 120}={\rm 100}+({\rm 100}\times 0.2)</math>.
*** If the user has a critical damage modifier such as {{ii|Infinity Edge}}, the damage amplifier is increased to 26%. The ability will thus critically strike for 126 damage: <math>{\rm 126}={\rm 100}+(({\rm 100}\times 0.2)\times (1+0.3))</math>.
*** If the target has critical strike damage reduction such as {{iis|Randuin's Omen}} {{nie|Resilience}}, which counts as a critical damage modifier for {{sbc|Cinderbloom}}, the damage amplifier is reduced to 14%. The ability will thus critically strike for 114 damage: <math>{\rm 114}={\rm 100}+(({\rm 100}\times 0.2)\times (1+(-0.3)))</math>.
*** If the user has {{ii|Infinity Edge}} and the target has {{ii|Randuin's Omen}}, the damage amplifier is reduced to {{fd|18.2}}%, which is only slightly lower than the base amplifier of 20% due to the multiplicative nature of the modifiers. The ability will thus critically strike for {{fd|118.2}} damage: <math>118.2=100+100\times 0.2\times (1+0.3)\times(1-0.3)</math>.
** If the damage dealt already critically strikes, {{sbc|Cinderbloom}} will stack multiplicatively with the critical strike damage multiplier.
*** For example, if a damage instance critically strikes for 200% damage, {{sbc|Cinderbloom}} will stack with that multiplier multiplicatively for a total critical strike of 240% damage: <math>{\rm 2.4}={\rm 2}\times 1.2</math>.
* {{sbc|Cinderbloom}} affects the damage dealt by all sources except [[summoner spell]]s.
* {{sbc|Cinderbloom}} does not apply to damage dealt against [[structures]].
* {{sbc|Cinderbloom}} only activates on damage instances against targets that are already below {{as|40% of their '''maximum''' health}}.
