* The item was named after user [https://www.op.gg/summoners/euw/Lord%20Dominik-001 Lord Dominik], who was known as one of the community lead of the CIS fan-site.
* Before [[V10.23]], this item was the same as {{ii|Last Whisper|image=Last Whisper item old2.png}} before being reworked on patch [[V5.22]]. The only difference is that it was {{g|500}} gold more expensive.
* The item's icon is reused for the removed [[Teamfight Tactics]] item {{TFT Item|Repeating Crossbow}}.
* This item was featured in [[Katarina: Issue 4]].
