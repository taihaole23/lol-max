|notes =
* {{sbc|Electrospark}} deals {{tip|proc damage}} and is tagged as {{tip|area damage|area of effect}} without applying {{tip|spell effects}}.
* The damage dealt by {{sbc|Electrospark}} is not affected by on-hit damage modifiers.

* The lightning from {{sbc|Electrospark}} consists of individual parts as it travels from unit to unit, each one firing after a delay of {{fd|0.05}} seconds.
* As with all other {{tip|Energized}} effects, {{sbc|Electrospark}} will activate and trigger against [[structures]]. It will not target [[structures]] if they are not the entities attacked.

|trivia =
* {{ii|Statikk Shiv}} is named after [[James 'Statikk' Bach]], a Senior Game Designer at Riot.
* Prior to [[V5.22]], '''Statikk Shiv''' had the same animation effects and sound as {{cai|Electrical Surge|Kennen}}.
** When activating both {{cai|Electrical Surge|Kennen}} and '''Statikk Shiv''' passive within a small enough window, whichever was casted second will not have the sound effects replay (''due to the previous exact copy of the sound just previously playing'').
** The only difference is that '''Statikk Shiv''' applies the effect at the seven closest targets, whereas {{cai|Electrical Surge|Kennen}} targets all [[unit]]s that are affected by {{ai|Mark of the Storm|Kennen}}.
