|notes =
* {{sbc|Immolate}} deals {{tip|proc damage}} and is tagged as {{tip|persistent damage|periodic}} without applying {{tip|spell effects}}.
* {{sbc|Immolate}} will trigger from its own damage, burning infinitely so long and there is an enemy in range.
* {{sbc|Immolate}} is an [[auto-targeted]] effect.
* {{sbc|Immolate's}} current damage per second is displayed on the item's icon as a counter which dynamically updates.
** This is exclusive to '''Sunfire Aegis''' and is not present in {{ii|Hollow Radiance}}; it is vestigial functionality due to the former's {{sbc|Immolate}} effect dealing a variable amount of damage in a previous iteration.
* Against {{tip|minions}}, it would deal an increased {{as|{{ap|20*1.5}}|magic damage}} {{as|(+ {{ap|1.5*1.5}}% '''bonus''' health)|health}} {{as|magic damage}} per tick and against {{tip|monsters}}, it would deal an increased {{as|{{ap|20*1.8}}|magic damage}} {{as|(+ {{ap|1.5*1.8}}% '''bonus''' health)|health}} {{as|magic damage}} per tick.
* The aura increases if the user increases in [[size]], but is not impacted by their '''base''' size.
** The aura cannot get reduced below the base radius by negative size modifiers.
* Because the [[aura]] must reach an enemy's center to damage them, some melee champions will not apply the effect at their maximum attack range against champions or {{tip|monsters}} with a large enough collision radius (for example the {{ui|Dragon}} or {{ui|Rift Herald}}).
