|notes =
* {{sbc|Cleave}} deals {{tip|proc damage}} and is tagged as {{tip|area damage|area of effect}} without applying {{tip|spell effects}}.
* {{sbc|Cleave}} strikes all targets in the cone simultaneously, and is not a {{tip|projectile}}.
* {{sbc|Cleave's}} on-hit damage benefits from {{stil|life steal}}.
** The damage dealt by the area of effect cone does not.
* {{sbc|Cleave's}} on-hit damage will apply to [[structures]] but its cone will not trigger while attacking them.
* {{sbc|Cleave}} will trigger even if the basic attack does not deal damage, such as if the target is {{tip|invulnerable}}.
* A single attack can cause a maximum of 10 {{sbc|Cleave}} splashes.
